## 🛒 Core Domains & Retail Standards

### 1. ⚡ Permanent QuickAction Speed Keys vs. Full SKU Catalog Division
- **Dedicated QuickAction Favorites Widget**: In high-velocity retail and quick-serve counters, the top 8-12 high-velocity items (Kantong Belanja, Kopi signature, Mineral Water, Best-seller pastries) must be a **permanent, fixed quick-action dock/widget**, NEVER buried or mixed ambiguously inside the scrolling inventory catalog.
- **Deep Catalog & SKU Search**: The full catalog with category filters, barcode lookup, and pagination exists separately for searching thousands of non-pinned SKUs without cluttering the main cashier hit-zone.

### 2. 📦 Rapid Barcode Scanning & Hardware Gun Integration
- **Zero-Latency Scanner**: Support for physical USB/Bluetooth 1D/2D barcode scanners (auto Enter key append) and on-screen camera scanner.
- **Instant SKU Match**: Scanning a barcode immediately adds +1 qty to the cart without opening unnecessary modal sheets.

### 3. 💵 Rapid Cash Tender & Accurate Change Velocity
- **Quick Cash Presets**: Exact change (`Uang Pas`), `20k`, `50k`, `100k`, `200k` with instantaneous large-font change calculation.
- **Stock Depletion & Low-Stock Alerts**: Real-time decrement of retail inventory.

## 🎯 Review & Evaluation Protocol

Whenever auditing, reviewing, or designing Retail & POS workflows:
1. **⚡ Speed of Execution (< 3 seconds per item)**: Bisakah kasir memproses 5 item dalam waktu kurang dari 10 detik?
2. **🧱 Clear Spatial Separation**: Apakah widget permanen QuickAction terpisah tegas dari katalog pencarian SKU?
3. **📊 Inventory & Barcode Flow**: Apakah alur scan barcode SKU berjalan instan tanpa modal membingungkan?
