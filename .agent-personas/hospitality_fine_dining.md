## 🍷 Core Domains & Hospitality Standards

### 1. 🍽️ Multi-Course Pacing & Kitchen Synchronization (Course Timing)
- **Course Management (`Hold` & `Fire Course`)**: In fine dining and modern bistros, food cannot be dumped all at once. The POS/KDS must support structured multi-course pacing:
  - *Course 1 (Amuse-Bouche & Appetizer)*
  - *Course 2 (Entrée / Main Course - Hold until fired by Server)*
  - *Course 3 (Dessert & Digestif / Specialty Coffee)*
- **Course Firing Triggers**: Maitre D' or Server can tap `🔥 Fire Course 2` from table view, automatically triggering kitchen tickets with exact time elapsed.

### 2. 👑 VIP Guest Recognition, Allergen Safeguards & CRM Profiles
- **Zero-Tolerance Allergen Protocol**: Shellfish, Peanut, Celiac/Gluten, Dairy/Lactose, and Dietary restrictions (Halal, Kosher, Vegan) must be prominently highlighted on Seat-Level badges with soft rose/amber alert indicators on KDS and POS.
- **Guest Profiling & Preferences**: Storing guest favorite seating areas (Window, Booth, Private Dining Room), preferred water (San Pellegrino Sparkling vs Aqua Panna Still), and VIP loyalty tier.

### 3. 🍷 Sommelier, Wine Pairing & Beverage Temperature
- **Vintage & Pairing Notes**: Wine list with vintage years, grape varietal, cellar bin location, and recommended dish pairings.
- **Glassware & Temperature Integrity**: Serving temperature indicators for staff.

### 4. 💳 Discrete High-End Billing & Seat-Level Split
- **Seat-Level Accuracy**: Each seat (`Seat 1`, `Seat 2`, `Seat 3`, `Seat 4`) owns its items, allowing 1-tap partial seat checkout or unified host billing.
- **Discretion & Open-Tab Hospitality**: High-end guests rarely pay per item at the counter. They expect an **Open Tab** settled at the end of the evening with discrete folder presentation or digital link.

### 5. 🕯️ Low-Light Ambient UI & Floor Ergonomics
- **Ambient Contrast**: Waiters in dim, ambient dining rooms need clear, soft dark-mode interfaces that don't glow blindingly into guests' faces.
- **Tactile Floor Map**: Instant visual clarity on table status: *Seated (Appetizer)* ➔ *Main Course In Progress* ➔ *Dessert* ➔ *Billed / Open Tab*.
